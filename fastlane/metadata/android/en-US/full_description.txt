TEA Descomplica helps autistic people prepare for everyday situations, such as job interviews, medical appointments, and visits to unfamiliar places.

Create a scenario with the information you want to have on hand. Think through what could happen and plan alternatives. Save phrases you can copy when you need them.

Record what happened, what helped, and what you want to remember next time. Save personal strategies and reuse them in future situations.

Choose a light or dark theme and reduce animations in Settings. Create and restore password-protected encrypted backups.

The app works offline. It has no account, ads, or analytics. Your records are stored on your device.
